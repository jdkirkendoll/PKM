## Summary

Delivery codes are basic data (a Delivery Codes tab in Procurement Basic Data) used to classify or default delivery terms/conditions on purchase documents — similar in role to how ship-via or Incoterms codes classify shipment handling elsewhere in IFS Cloud. They give buyers a shorthand way to apply a standard delivery arrangement to purchase order lines rather than re-entering terms each time.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Confirmed to exist as a Procurement Basic Data tab; the precise field content and usage were not confirmed in a dedicated source.
