## Summary

Payment Info is the set of fields on the supplier record that determine how and when the supplier is paid: payment method, payment priority, planned payment delay, and bank/EDI details for electronic payments. It works together with payment address and payment terms (entered elsewhere on the Invoice tab) to fully define the supplier's payment behavior in Accounts Payable.
