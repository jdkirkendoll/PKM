## Summary

An inspection code indicates that purchased parts on a receipt line must be inspected and determines the sample size to inspect — either a fixed quantity or a percentage of the received quantity (if a fixed quantity exceeds what was actually ordered, the system automatically reduces it and warns you). A purchase order line with a receive case that demands inspection carries an inspection code, and the same codes can also be used within IFS/Quality Management control plans to determine sample sizes there.
