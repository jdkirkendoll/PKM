# Define Disposition Codes

## Summary

Disposition Codes describe the fate of a nonconforming part or material once it's been reviewed — common outcomes include approved for use as-is, returned to the supplier, reworked, or scrapped. They're used both on MRB (Material Review Board) cases, where a case can result in a single disposition or a mixed disposition across quantities, and on Non Conformance Reports.
