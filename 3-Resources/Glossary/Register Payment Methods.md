## Summary

Registers the methods by which an employee can be paid (e.g. bank transfer, check) for use in Employee Payment processes such as Payment in Full, an alternative to Payroll Transfer used to pay the difference between amounts already paid and an authorized expense sheet's total when IFS/Employee Payment is installed.
