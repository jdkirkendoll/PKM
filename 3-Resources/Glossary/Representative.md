# Representative

## Summary

A Representative is the CRM basic-data record for a person who works for the organization and is responsible for a step of the sales process — a sales representative connected to leads, opportunities and business activities. When a business activity is created, the representative connected to the current user is automatically retrieved into the Main Representative field, and additional representatives can be added to the record's Representatives section. In IFS Cloud's CRM analysis model, representatives are exposed via the Business Representative and Main Representative dimensions (each built as code + name), both sourced from the same underlying DIM_BUSINESS_REP dimension.
