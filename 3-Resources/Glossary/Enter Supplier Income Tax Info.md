## Summary

Income tax info is entered on a supplier record for jurisdictions that require withholding or reporting on payments to certain suppliers (e.g., 1099 reporting in the US, or similar withholding-tax regimes elsewhere) — it typically includes the supplier's tax identification data and any withholding classification. This flags the supplier so payments trigger the correct tax withholding/reporting behavior at invoice and payment time.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The general purpose (income/withholding tax classification tied to the supplier) is standard ERP practice, but the exact IFS Cloud field set for this tab was not independently confirmed in a source.
