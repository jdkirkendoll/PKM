## Summary

Buyers are basic data (set up in Procurement Basic Data) identifying the people responsible for purchase requisitions and orders — releasing, sending, and following up on them. A buyer can also be used as an authorizer type in purchase authorization routing (only the buyer on a document can authorize a "buyer authorizer" step), and buyers can be filtered against in authorization rules and central-purchasing security setup.
