## Summary

This is the umbrella activity-diagram-level entry for the whole "Enter Supplier" business process — creating a new supplier record so it can be used on requests for quotation, purchase requisitions, and purchase orders. Suppliers can be entered from several places (the Supplier window in IFS Invoicing, or in IFS Procurement), and the individual tabs/sub-activities under it (General, Address, Purchase, Invoice, Payment, Communication Method, Tax, Message, etc.) are each broken out as their own BDR rows in this table.
