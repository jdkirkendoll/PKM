# Define External Work Centre Cost

## Summary

For outside (subcontracted) operations, IFS Cloud does not store cost on the work centre record itself — costs for outside operations are instead defined and maintained on the dedicated Outside Operation Costs page, tied to the purchase part/supplier price used to buy the operation. This feeds Cost Elements 140 (Planned Purchase Price) and 160 (Purchase Additional Cost Amount), sourced from Purchasing rather than from the Work Center's Costs tab. The structural separation lets external, supplier-driven operation pricing be maintained independently of internal work centre fixed rates and overheads.
