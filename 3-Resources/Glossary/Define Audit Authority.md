# Define Audit Authority

## Summary

An Audit Authority is the specific body — a named certification body, regulator, or customer — that an audit or Reference Standard is performed for or against, categorized under an [[3-Resources/Glossary/Define Audit Authority Type|Audit Authority Type]]. Compliance Planning uses this to identify which reference standards are relevant to each business area and to track planned audits against a specific standard/authority.
