## Summary

This is the purchasing-specific data entered on the Purchase tab of the Supplier page — currency, purchase terms, default buyer/coordinator, price effective date parameter, and whether the supplier can be used as a template for quick registration of other suppliers. It's what makes a supplier usable on requests for quotation, purchase requisitions, and purchase orders, as distinct from the invoicing-side data entered on the Invoice tab.
