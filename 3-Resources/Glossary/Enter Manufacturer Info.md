## Summary

Manufacturer information links a purchase part to the actual manufacturer(s) that make it and records the manufacturer's own part number, entered on the Part Manufacturer tab of the Part page. From there, manufacturers already defined for the part can be copied onto the Manufacturers tab of the Supplier for Purchase Part page, with the first one copied automatically becoming the preferred manufacturer for that supplier relationship — useful when a part can be sourced from a supplier who carries multiple manufacturers' equivalents.
