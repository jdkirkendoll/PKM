## Summary

This is the core step of setting up a purchase part's general data — part number, description, purchase unit of measure and its conversion factor to the inventory unit, and other header-level attributes on the Purchase Part page. For a purchased inventory part, IFS Cloud's Part Wizard can create the purchase part automatically once the inventory part is entered; for a purchased non-inventory part there is no inventory part, so the information must be entered directly on the Purchase Part tab. This general info underpins every other purchase-part activity (pricing, supplier connections, characteristics, etc.).
