# Create Category 2

## Summary

Category 2 is a second, independent user-defined classification code that can be assigned to a Sales Contract alongside Category 1, giving the business a further dimension for grouping, filtering, and reporting on contracts (for instance, Category 1 might capture business line while Category 2 captures region or risk tier). The two categories are typically set up and used together as a simple two-axis classification scheme.
