## Summary

Most likely corresponds to Resource Demand — the bottom level of the confirmed Service Project hierarchy (Project > Request/Request Quotation > Scope > Task > Resource Demand) in IFS Cloud's Work Processing documentation. A Resource Demand is what actually needs to be scheduled/allocated: it's driven by the Task's planned start and duration, and demand labels show Planned Quantity vs. Assigned Count so a planner can see fulfillment at a glance. Each resource demand equates to one activity sent to the Dynamic Scheduling Engine.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Resource Demand is confirmed as the requirement-level object in this hierarchy; whether the BDR's exact term "Requirement" maps one-to-one onto Resource Demand (versus being a distinct, undocumented concept) is inferred rather than confirmed by name.
