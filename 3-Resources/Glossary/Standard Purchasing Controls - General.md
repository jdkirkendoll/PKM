# Standard Purchasing Controls - General

## Summary

This covers the general, non-hire-specific purchasing basic data that has to exist before any purchase order flow — including hire-in — can run: purchase groups (used to group purchase parts and to apply discount/additional-cost terms per supplier), and purchase authorization basic data (authorization routing templates connected to a site/purchase group combination, which determine whether a purchase requisition or order needs approval before it can proceed). Getting these general controls right is a prerequisite for the hire-specific purchasing setup layered on top of it.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The purchase group and purchase authorization mechanisms are confirmed in IFS documentation, but no page uses the exact label "Standard Purchasing Controls - General" — this entry summarizes the general purchasing basic data those specific hire-related items build on.
