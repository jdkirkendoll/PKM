## Summary

Expense rules define how allowances, deductions, and reimbursements are calculated on an employee's expense sheet (e.g. for meals, accommodation, or mileage), and expense codes are the individual line-item categories (such as "Hotel" or "Taxi") an employee selects when registering an expense — expense rules can be connected to an employee category so the correct rule applies automatically without per-employee setup.
