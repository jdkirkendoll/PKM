## Summary

A pallet inspection location is where pallet-handled goods are moved for quality inspection, mirroring the role of the standard inspection location but scoped to whole pallets rather than individual parts. As with normal inspection, results can be reported regardless of exactly where the pallet currently sits (arrival, inspection, or stock), but a dedicated pallet inspection location keeps pallet-level QA organized and physically separated from piece-pick stock.
