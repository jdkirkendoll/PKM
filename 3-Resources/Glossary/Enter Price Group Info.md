## Summary

A price group is basic data used to organize purchase parts (or supplier-for-purchase-part records) for pricing purposes, including price-including-tax handling — when the Use Price Incl Tax option is enabled on a Supplier for Purchase Part record, price and additional-cost fields can be entered tax-inclusive and the system calculates the tax-exclusive amounts using the applicable tax code. Assigning a purchase part to the right price group ensures the correct pricing and tax treatment is applied when purchase order lines and price lists reference it.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The tax-inclusive pricing mechanics are confirmed; the broader role of "price group" as a grouping concept for purchase parts is inferred by analogy with the equivalent sales price group concept and was not independently confirmed for purchasing.
