## Summary
A Develop Level categorizes the lifecycle phase an engineering part revision is progressing through — from analysis/design toward manufacturing/maintenance — with examples such as analysis, design, prototyping, testing, or manufacturing. Develop levels are configured in the Product Basic Data window's Develop Levels tab, and a part revision's develop level can be set or changed on the Engineering Part Revision/Revision tab. A revision's develop level can still be changed while it's in Development status with an active part structure, but once the revision is released the develop level becomes fixed and can no longer be edited, which protects released data from unintended lifecycle changes.
