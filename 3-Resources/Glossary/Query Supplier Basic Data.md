## Summary

This is the ability to search and filter across supplier basic data — by supplier code, name, statistic group, country, or other attributes — to find and verify supplier records, similar to the multi-supplier search capability used in IFS Business Reporter quick reports. It supports both day-to-day lookups (finding the right supplier to use on a document) and periodic data-quality review of the supplier master.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> General querying capability is a standard IFS Cloud pattern across master-data pages; a dedicated source describing a specific "Query Supplier Basic Data" screen was not found.
