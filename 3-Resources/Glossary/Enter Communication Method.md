## Summary

Communication method defines how purchasing documents (requests for quotation, purchase orders, reminders) are sent to a supplier — for example printed and mailed, faxed, e-mailed as a PDF attachment, or exchanged via EDI. Setting the right communication method on the supplier record determines what happens automatically when a document is printed/sent, including under automatic order processing.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Based on IFS Cloud's general document-transmission options (print, e-mail, EDI) referenced across procurement documentation; a dedicated source describing this exact supplier tab was not found.
