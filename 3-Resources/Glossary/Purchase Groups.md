## Summary

Purchase groups are basic data used to categorize purchase parts (and requisition/order lines) for statistical reporting, queries, and printouts, and they can also be used as control types in posting control. Authorization rules can be scoped to specific purchase groups (or set to apply regardless of purchase group), making purchase groups a common way to route different categories of spend through different approval rules or reporting buckets.
