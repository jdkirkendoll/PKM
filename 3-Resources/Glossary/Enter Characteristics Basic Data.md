## Summary
Characteristics basic data consists of two related pieces: Classes (a class is a named collection of attributes, e.g. "hard disk," "monitor," or "PC") and Attributes (individual properties such as color, length, pressure, or weight, each tied to a unit via Unit Codes and Relationships). Once a class is assigned to an engineering part or product function, its full set of attributes is copied onto that object as a template, and values can then be entered for each attribute — enabling dynamic specification templates, itemized part properties, and Best-Fit Search for parts matching given criteria. Numeric characteristic attributes can also be rolled up and calculated across a whole product structure (e.g. total weight). Characteristics must be approved (toggled from Not Approved to Approved) before a part carrying them can be released.
