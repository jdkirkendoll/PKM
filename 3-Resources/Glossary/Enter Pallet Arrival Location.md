## Summary

When parts are handled on pallets, receipt needs a dedicated pallet arrival location — the equivalent of the standard arrival location, but sized and configured for pallet-level (rather than piece-level) putaway. Pallets created for pallet-handled parts are placed here when the arrival is first registered, before any inspection or move to a pallet stock location.
