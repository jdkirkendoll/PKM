# Field

## Summary

A Field is the most basic IFS Cloud Web control for entering and displaying a single attribute's value, used inside a list, group, or selector. Its visual appearance is largely auto-generated from the underlying attribute's data type — e.g. a not-null read-only Boolean becomes a Badge, a not-null editable Boolean becomes a Toggle Button, a nullable Boolean or enumeration becomes a Drop-down List, and Date/Time/Timestamp attributes get a Date Time Picker automatically — while other variants (Address Field, Computed Field, Currency/Measure, Date Range Field, Free Text, List of Values, Multiline, Signature, Static) must be added manually. Common customization properties include `label`, `required`, `editable`, `visible`, `searchable`, `filterlabel`, `format`, `size`, and `multiline`.
