## Summary

General supplier info covers the core identifying data entered on the General tab of the Supplier page when a supplier is first created — supplier ID, name, corporate form, and country, among other header-level fields. This is the mandatory starting point of supplier setup: the supplier record needs values in at least the General and Address tabs before it can be used for invoice entry or purchasing.
