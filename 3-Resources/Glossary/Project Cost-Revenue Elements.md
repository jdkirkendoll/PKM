# Project Cost/Revenue Elements

## Summary

Project cost/revenue elements are user-definable categories used to break down project cost and revenue for planning and follow-up purposes — for example grouping all accounts that capture material cost under a "Material" element. They are defined once per company (all projects in the company share the same set) and are mandatory basic data for IFS Project Management. For hire billing, cost/revenue elements let hire-related charges and income be reported and analyzed by category (e.g. equipment hire cost vs. hire revenue) across every project rather than only at the raw account level.
