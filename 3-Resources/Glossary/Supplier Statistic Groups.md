## Summary

A supplier statistic group classifies suppliers for reporting and control purposes (a Suppl Stat Grp tab in Procurement Basic Data). It is used as a criterion on purchase order authorization rules — a rule can be scoped to specific supplier statistic groups so, for instance, all suppliers in a higher-risk group route through a stricter authorization routing template, or the rule can be set to apply regardless of which group a supplier belongs to.
