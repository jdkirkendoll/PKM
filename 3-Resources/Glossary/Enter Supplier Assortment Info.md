## Summary

A supplier assortment groups purchase parts (inventory-registered, non-inventory-registered, or no-number parts) that share the same discount and additional-cost terms from one specific supplier, so a purchase agreement can define pricing once for the whole assortment instead of line by line. Supplier assortments are entered as basic data in Purchasing and are always valid across all sites on the agreement (never site-specific); they can also use assortment step pricing, where the discount and additional cost improve once cumulative purchases from that assortment cross a minimum-amount threshold.
