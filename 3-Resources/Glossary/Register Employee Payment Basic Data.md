## Summary

Sets up the basic data needed for the Employee Payment component to pay employees directly (rather than only via Payroll) — including bank account details (EMPPAYACC), default payment method (EMPPAYMETH), and bank identifier codes (BIC) — used, for example, to pay travel expense advances and reimbursements.
