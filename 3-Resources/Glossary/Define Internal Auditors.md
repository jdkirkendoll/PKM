# Define Internal Auditors

## Summary

This basic data registers people within the organization who are qualified to carry out Quality Audits internally, as distinct from external auditors brought in from outside the company. IFS documentation defines a quality audit as being carried out by "an internal or external quality auditor" — this activity maintains the list of who counts as the former.
