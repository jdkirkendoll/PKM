# Posting Controls In the Order group for Hire Out

## Summary

"Hire out" (rent-out) is when the company rents its own equipment to a customer, tracked through a customer order. Order Group is the corresponding control type on the sales side to Purchase Group on the buying side — it lets revenue postings for hired-out equipment be routed to different accounts depending on the order group the customer order line belongs to. This must be configured before go-live so that hire revenue from customer rental orders posts to the correct income accounts automatically.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The purchase-group/order-group control type pattern and general rent-out flow (customer order must be Released with reserved items) are confirmed in IFS documentation, but no page describing "posting controls in the order group for hire out" by that exact name was found.
