## Summary

Employment types classify an employment period for informational and statistical purposes — common examples are Permanent, Seasonal, Temporary, and Fixed-term employment. They're linked to the employment period and used in areas like workforce/demographic reporting and, where a Payroll component is installed, may carry extended payroll-specific information.
