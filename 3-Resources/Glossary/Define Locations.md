## Summary

Defines the physical locations (with geo-coordinates) that activities can take place at or resources can be based from, within IFS Cloud's Planning and Scheduling Optimization (PSO) suite. "Locations," "Location," and "Location Addresses" are all confirmed pages in the PSO documentation. Valid geo-coordinates on locations (via map positions) are required for the Dynamic Scheduling Engine (DSE) to calculate travel and for the Appointment Booking Engine (ABE) to book appointments — a Work Task without coordinates cannot be appointment booked.
