## Summary

A normal career path is a predefined progression of positions/jobs that is typical for a role, used as one of the factors (alongside stated career aspirations, budget, and required skills) that a manager and employee take into account when building the employee's individual [[3-Resources/Glossary/Register Career Planning Periods|Career Plan]].
