## Summary

Supplier agreement (blanket) types are basic data set up in Purchasing to group and categorize supplier agreements for querying and reporting purposes, similar to how purchase groups categorize parts. The same types can be used on both purchase agreements and blanket purchase orders, giving buyers a way to filter or group agreements beyond just supplier or status.
