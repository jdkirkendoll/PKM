## Summary

Positions form the position structure — the chain of authority within a company or matrix organization, with higher-ranking positions above their subordinates. A position structure is tied to its corresponding organization structure via a shared structure ID, and employees are assigned to positions to determine reporting lines, access, and job/salary information.
