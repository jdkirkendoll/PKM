# Stage

## Summary

Stage tracks where a Lead currently sits in its qualification journey. In IFS Cloud, a lead's stage is not a free-standing field but comes from the CRM Process (a qualification process built from a process template) assigned to the lead — as the sales/marketing team completes each stage's actions, the Stage field updates automatically to reflect progress. Stages are configurable per company (a simple example setup uses Contact, Lead, Prospect, Opportunity, Customer), and each stage in a process template can also carry a default probability. Stage is used to report on and drive lead-nurturing workflows before a lead converts into a Business Opportunity.
