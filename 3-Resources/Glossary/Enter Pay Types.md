## Summary

Registers the types of additional pay (bonuses, allowances, one-off payments, etc.) that can be granted to an employee outside their base salary, which are then scheduled using [[3-Resources/Glossary/Enter Periods of Additional Pay Types|Enter Periods of Additional Pay Types]] and transferred to Payroll.
