## Summary

Defines a Team as a schedulable grouping of resources — conceptually close to the confirmed Crew resource concept in this suite, where crew members are visualized in the Scheduling Workbench with their own validity period and crew demand-type tasks can be scheduled against crew resources by the Dynamic Scheduling Engine. Whether "Team" here is simply this suite's term for what the underlying engine calls a "Crew," or a distinct grouping used only for reporting/administration, wasn't confirmed.

> [!note] Confidence: low
> Crew resources are confirmed as a real, schedulable concept in this suite; the specific "Team" terminology and object used in this BDR were not found under that name in any source.
