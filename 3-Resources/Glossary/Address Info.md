## Summary

Address Info covers the mandatory address and address-type data entered on the Address tab of a supplier's General Address Info — the supplier's main registered address, distinct from any separate purchase or payment addresses that may be entered for specific transaction flows. It is one of the two tabs (with General) that must be populated before the supplier record is usable.
