## Summary

Job grades classify jobs/positions into a ranked structure (e.g. by seniority or pay level) that can be linked to a salary range, so that an employee assigned to a job can be related to an appropriate compensation band. Job grades are also used in Human Resource Planning as basic data for competence and workforce analysis.
