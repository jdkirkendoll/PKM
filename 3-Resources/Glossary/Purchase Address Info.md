## Summary

Purchase Address Info is the address a supplier should be sent purchase orders and related purchasing correspondence at, which can differ from the supplier's general registered address (for example, a separate sales office or order-desk address). It is entered on the Purchase tab of the Supplier page and is what purchase order printing/sending defaults to for that supplier.
