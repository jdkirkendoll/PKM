## Summary

Property codes extend the standard data model with additional, company-configurable fields at the Company, Office, Position, and Job level (for example, controlling behavior like automatic employee-number generation, self-access rules, or payroll integration flags). IFS Cloud ships a large set of system-defined property codes covering Employees and Organizational Definitions, Schedules and Rules, Time Reporting, Training Administration, Travel Expenses, Employee Payment, and Project Reporting.
