## Summary

Week types identify the different working-time patterns represented by the weeks of a cycle schedule (for example, a three-week rotating shift might use one week type/color for mornings, one for evenings, and one for nights). A week type can be applied to individual schedule rows, not just full weeks, and is also used to flag non-working days so they display correctly in week-mode Availability Planning.
