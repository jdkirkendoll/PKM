## Summary

If IFS Invoicing is installed, supplier invoice info is entered on the Invoice tab of the Supplier page — company, supplier type/group, currency, payment term, planned payment delay, authorizer, preliminary code, national bank code, EDI default values, and matching tolerance information. Options here also control whether payment plans auto-authorize at final posting and whether a payment reference number is mandatory at invoice entry, so this tab effectively configures how the supplier's invoices are processed once received.
