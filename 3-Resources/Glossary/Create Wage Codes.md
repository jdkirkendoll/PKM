## Summary

In the IFS/Payroll component, wage codes (sometimes called wage types) define the individual pay elements — basic pay, overtime premiums, allowances, deductions — that can appear on an employee's payslip, each with its own calculation, tax, and posting behavior. This mirrors, but is configured separately from, the Time and Attendance wage codes described under [[3-Resources/Glossary/Enter Wage Code|Enter Wage Code]].
