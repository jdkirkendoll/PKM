## Summary

The payment address is the address used when sending payment-related correspondence (such as remittance advice or checks) to a supplier, which can be different from both the supplier's general address and its purchase address — for example, when a supplier's accounts-receivable department is at a different location than its sales office. It is entered as part of the supplier's payment setup alongside payment method and payment priority.
