## Summary

An authorization group is a named set of several individual authorizers who together act as one entry in an authorization routing template. When a group is used in a template, only one member of the group needs to authorize the requisition or purchase order for that step to be satisfied — the others do not also need to sign off. Groups let a company route approval to "any purchasing manager" or "any of the finance team" rather than naming one specific person, so authorization does not stall if an individual authorizer is unavailable.
