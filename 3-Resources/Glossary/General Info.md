## Summary

General Info is the base tab on the Supplier page covering identifying and classification data for a supplier — name, corporate form, country, supplier type/group — that other tabs (Address, Purchase, Invoice, Payment) build on. A supplier record needs values in the General and Address tabs at minimum before it can be used at invoice entry or on a purchase document.
