# Assign Cost Estimator to one or many Parts

## Summary

This activity connects a previously defined Cost Estimator (Person) to one or more inventory parts on their Part Cost records in IFS Cloud Costing. The assignment is organizational rather than a security control — anyone with the correct IFS permission set can still perform a Cost Rollup on the part regardless of who is assigned as its Cost Estimator. Its practical value is on the Part Costs overview page, where the Cost Estimator field lets a person filter the list down to only the parts they are personally responsible for costing.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> No dedicated official IFS documentation page describing bulk/multi-part assignment was found; this is inferred from an IFS Community discussion of the Cost Estimator field on individual Part Cost records, which does not explicitly confirm a separate bulk-assignment UI for "one or many" parts.
