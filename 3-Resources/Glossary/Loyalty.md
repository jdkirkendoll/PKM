# Loyalty

## Summary

Loyalty is a basic-data classification value that can be recorded against a Lead, used to categorize how loyal or engaged the account/contact is expected to be — similar in nature to the other configurable Lead classification LOVs (Potential, Source, Market, Forms of Business).

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> No IFS documentation page or community thread describing this exact field was found in the local corpus or on the web (searches only surfaced unrelated marketing material about "customer loyalty" as a business outcome, and third-party loyalty-program integrations). This entry is inferred from its position alongside the other Lead classification fields in the BDR list.
