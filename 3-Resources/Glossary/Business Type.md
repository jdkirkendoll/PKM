# Business Type

## Summary

Business Type indicates whether a Business Opportunity is regarding new sales ("New Business") or repetitive/recurring sales ("Existing Business"), such as regular services or rentals. The field always defaults to "New" and has no automatic connection to other fields, so it must be changed intentionally when it applies to existing/recurring business. Business Type is also one of the parameters (alongside Representative, Account, Account Category, Country, Market and Company) that can be weighted to determine which default CRM sales process is automatically assigned when an opportunity is created.
