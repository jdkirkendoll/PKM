## Summary

A delivery pattern is basic data (created under Procurement Basic Data) that describes a recurring delivery schedule from a supplier — for example, which days of the week deliveries normally arrive. Connecting a delivery pattern to a purchase part/supplier combination lets the system suggest or validate expected delivery dates for planned purchase requisitions and orders for that part, which is particularly useful for parts bought regularly on a predictable cadence.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The general purpose of delivery patterns is well established from the Procurement Basic Data structure, but the exact mechanics of how a pattern is connected to a part were not confirmed in a dedicated source.
