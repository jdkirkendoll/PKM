# Forms of Business

## Summary

Forms of Business is a basic-data classification value entered on a Lead, alongside sibling fields like Potential, Source and Market, used to categorize the type or nature of business the lead represents for segmentation and reporting purposes.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> No IFS documentation page or community thread describing this exact field was found in the local corpus or on the web. This entry is inferred from its position alongside the other Lead classification fields (Potential, Source, Market) in the BDR list, which are all simple configurable LOVs maintained in CRM basic data.
