# Characteristic Family

## Summary

A Configuration Family is a collection of configuration characteristics shared by a group of configurable base parts — for example, a "Doors" family might contain Exterior Doors and Interior Doors, which share common characteristics such as material and size. A part must be connected to a Configuration Family before any characteristics can be selected for its configuration, making the family the foundation both the Sales Configurator (front office) and back-office Structure/Routing Rules build on. The Configure By Category option lets a family's characteristics be grouped and displayed by category during configuration.
