## Summary

A supplier assortment groups purchase parts (inventory, non-inventory, or no-number) that share the same discount and additional-cost terms from a single supplier, letting a purchase agreement define pricing once for the whole group instead of part by part — useful when the same supplier sells many similar items at a uniform discount. Supplier assortments are entered as Purchasing basic data, are valid across all sites on the agreement (never site-specific), can only be used on purchase agreements (not blanket purchase orders), and can carry their own step pricing that improves discount/additional cost once cumulative purchases pass a minimum amount.
