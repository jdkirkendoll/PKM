# Define Cost Centre Costs

## Summary

Once a Cost Centre exists, its actual budgeted cost amount is entered in the Cost Center window as the total cost for that cost centre under a given Cost Set. This is defined per time period — the system allows periods as short as daily, though a quarterly, semi-annual, or annual period is the recommended granularity. This total cost figure is the numerator in the activity cost driver rate calculation: it is divided by the estimated usage of each activity's cost driver (weighted by the activity's allocation factor from the cost centre) to arrive at the per-unit rate that gets attributed to parts.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Sourced from a training-exercise walkthrough of the Cost Center window ("enter the total cost for the cost center in cost set 2") rather than a dedicated reference page; exact field name and screen layout were not confirmed against current IFS Cloud documentation.
