## Summary

A supplier agreement (purchase agreement or blanket purchase order) moves through the statuses Planned, Negotiate, Active, Closed, and Canceled, and it must be in a status that allows purchasing — most often Active — before it can actually be used to price or release orders. An agreement can optionally be made effective while still in Negotiate status via the "Effective While Negotiating" option, which is what this activity configures: deciding which statuses are treated as purchasing-enabled for a given agreement or agreement type.
