## Summary

This data point sets threshold values used to monitor the health/quality of the schedule produced by the Dynamic Scheduling Engine (DSE) — for example acceptable levels of unallocated activities, SLA breaches, or travel efficiency — so that a degraded schedule surfaces as an alert to planners rather than going unnoticed. It sits alongside the exception- and error/warning-threshold data points as one of a family of monitoring/alerting configuration activities for this scheduling suite.
