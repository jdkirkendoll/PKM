## Summary

Likely defines a retention/loyalty rule that keeps a resource assigned to the same customer, site, or account across repeat visits — a common feature in field-service scheduling tools (sometimes called "continuity of service") intended to preserve familiarity between a technician and a customer/site, and to be respected as a soft or hard constraint by the scheduling engine's allocation.
