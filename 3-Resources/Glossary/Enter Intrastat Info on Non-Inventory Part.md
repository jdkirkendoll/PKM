## Summary

Non-inventory purchase parts and no-part order lines aren't stock-tracked, so they lack the inventory part record that normally carries customs statistics data. To have such a line included in the Intrastat report, the "Intrastat Affected" option must be selected directly on the order line, flagging it for inclusion in Intrastat collection even though there's no inventory part behind it.
