## Summary

An inventory part is a part that is stock-tracked at a site — quantities, locations, and valuation are maintained for it in inventory, as opposed to a non-inventory-registered part or a no-number part that isn't tracked this way. In Purchasing, a purchase part is layered on top of an inventory part (Inventory Part → Purchase Part → Supplier for Purchase Part) to add buying-specific data; many other BDR activities across Purchase Part, Intrastat, and Receipt reference "Inventory Part" because they are entering purchasing-, customs-, or location-related fields directly on the inventory part record (e.g., customs statistics number, net weight, default arrival/stock locations).
