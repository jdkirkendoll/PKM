## Summary

Automated invoicing (two-way matching / Evaluated Receipt Settlement) eliminates the need to wait for a supplier's invoice: enabling the Self-Billing option on the supplier makes it default onto new purchase order lines for that supplier, and when the arrival is registered, the system generates the supplier invoice itself based on received quantity and purchase price, then matches it automatically. It works best with a close supplier relationship and receive cases that don't require inspection, since automated invoicing doesn't handle scraps and returns; behavior also depends on the company's Ownership Transfer Point setting (Receipt into Arrival vs. Receipt into Inventory), which decides exactly when the self-billed invoice is created.
