## Summary

To use the Charge/No Charge outsourcing flow (where components are shipped to a supplier via a customer order, optionally charging the supplier for them), the supplier must also exist as a customer record, and that customer number must be connected to the supplier record. This connection is what lets the system automatically create a customer order — to reserve, pick, and ship the outsourced material — whenever a purchase order line indicates the components should go to that supplier.
