## Summary

Defines categories of Location (e.g. customer site, depot, warehouse) used within IFS Cloud's Planning and Scheduling Optimization (PSO) suite. "Location Type" is confirmed as a page in the PSO documentation's page list, alongside Location, Locations, Location Addresses, and Locality — these pages together support geo-coding and travel-distance/travel-time calculation between resources and activities.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Location Type's existence as a page is confirmed; the specific fields/behavior of that page were not detailed in the sources found.
