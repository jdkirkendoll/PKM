## Summary

For parts whose customs statistics number requires reporting in a unit other than kilograms (an "Intrastat Alternative Qty," e.g., pieces or liters), the Intrastat conversion factor records the manually calculated difference between that customs unit of measure and the part's normal inventory unit of measure. The Intrastat report uses this factor to compute the correct alternative quantity to report for that part.
