## Summary

Wage codes classify every type of hour or absence an employee can report — normal time, overtime, specific absence types, balance accrual/withdrawal, etc. — and drive both the Time Card-Day result and, ultimately, what is transferred to payroll. Wage codes are referenced throughout Schedules and Rules setup: in day type details, overtime rules, time balances (compensatory, switch, flex), and absence functions, and are also used directly in Expense Administration to classify travel/expense-related pay.
