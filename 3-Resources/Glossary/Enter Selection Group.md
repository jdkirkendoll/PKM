## Summary

Selection groups are a way of tagging employees so they can be selected together for schedules-and-rules processing (e.g. generating absence limits or running specific calculation rules for the group); the Get Selection Group calculation-formula function retrieves an employee's selection group on the current account date, and it is also one of the criteria available in Employee Selection Templates.
