## Summary

Invoice Info covers the supplier-level settings that control how that supplier's invoices are matched, authorized, and posted — company, currency, payment term, tolerance thresholds, and whether the supplier uses automated (self-billing) invoicing. It is also the specific setting checked for the automated invoicing (self-billing/ERS) flow: when Self-Billing is enabled on the supplier, that becomes the default on new purchase order lines, and receipts against those lines generate the supplier invoice automatically instead of waiting for one from the supplier.
