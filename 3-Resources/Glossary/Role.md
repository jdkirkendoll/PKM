# Role

## Summary

Role appears twice in the CRM BDR, describing two related but distinct basic-data LOVs. For a **Lead Contact**, Role (CRM Contact Role) defines the contact person's function in relation to that specific lead or business opportunity — examples include technical expert, sales administrator, budget approver, evaluator and decision maker; it's configured under Relationship Management > Customer Relationship Management > Basic Data, and is distinct from the more general, cross-entity "Contact Role" maintained in Application Base Setup. For a **Representative**, Role instead classifies the representative's own function on the sales team (e.g. account manager vs. sales support), maintained alongside Representative Group as CRM representative basic data.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The Lead Contact Role usage is confirmed by both the local corpus and an IFS Community thread. The Representative-side Role usage is inferred by analogy — no dedicated documentation for a representative-level Role field was found.
