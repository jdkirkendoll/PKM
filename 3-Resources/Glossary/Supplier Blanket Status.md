## Summary

A supplier agreement (blanket purchase order or purchase agreement) has one of five statuses: Planned (default on creation), Negotiate, Active, Closed, or Canceled. It can move freely between Planned, Negotiate, Active, or Closed depending on workflow, but once Canceled it cannot be changed further; the agreement is only effective (usable for pricing/purchasing) once Active, unless "Effective While Negotiating" is enabled, in which case Negotiate status is also usable.
