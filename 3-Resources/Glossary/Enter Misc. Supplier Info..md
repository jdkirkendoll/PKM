## Summary

This activity captures the miscellaneous supplier fields that don't fall under the main General, Address, Purchase, Invoice, or Payment tabs — things like supplier statistic group, property codes, or other classification and reference data used for reporting and control-type posting. It rounds out the supplier record after the core identifying, address, and financial tabs are complete.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> This is inferred as a catch-all for supplier fields not covered by the other, more specific BDR rows in this same group; no dedicated source enumerating an exact "Misc. Supplier Info" tab was found.
