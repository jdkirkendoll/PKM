## Summary

In IFS Cloud, a calendar is built from Day Types, Schedules, and Schedule Exceptions — schedules and exceptions are connected together to define which days are working days, holidays, or otherwise exceptional. For warehousing, calendars underpin worker schedules, determining which days and hours a warehouse worker is actually available to be assigned warehouse tasks.
