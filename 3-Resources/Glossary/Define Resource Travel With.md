## Summary

Likely defines which resources travel together as a unit (e.g. an apprentice riding with a lead technician, or crew members sharing one vehicle) so the scheduling engine accounts for shared travel time/cost rather than calculating separate independent journeys for each resource. This concept is adjacent to the confirmed crew-resource scheduling support in this suite, where crew members are visualized in the Scheduling Workbench with their validity period and crew demand-type tasks can be scheduled to crew resources.
