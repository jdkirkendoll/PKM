## Summary

Employment reduction periods track the reason for, and percentage of, a reduction in an employee's normal working hours over a defined period — e.g. Parental Leave, Military Service, Sickness, Sabbatical, or Education — expressed against the full-time equivalent hours. This activity registers the standard reduction-reason types available for use, which can also be linked to absence codes so a reduction period updates automatically when a matching absence is entered.
