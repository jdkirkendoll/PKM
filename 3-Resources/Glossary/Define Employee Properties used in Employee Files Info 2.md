## Summary

Property codes (as distinct from free fields) can also be defined and used in an employee context, appearing on the Employee Files/Properties tab, to hold additional configurable attributes about an employee — some system-defined property codes control application behavior (e.g. payroll integration, access defaults) rather than just storing information.
