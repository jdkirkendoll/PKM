## Summary

This is a read-oriented overview screen for reviewing the inventory locations (arrival, inspection, and stock locations) that goods can move through during receipt for a given site or part, rather than the detail page used to create/edit an individual location. It complements the location basic data covered in the existing Inventory Locations entry, letting warehouse and purchasing staff quickly check what default and alternate locations exist before troubleshooting a receipt.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Inferred from the naming pattern of other "Overview" screens in IFS Cloud and the arrival/inspection/stock location model in the Receipt documentation; a dedicated source confirming this exact screen's contents was not found.
