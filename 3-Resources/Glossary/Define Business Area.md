# Define Business Area

## Summary

A Business Area, in the context of Compliance Planning, is an organizational division that requires audit coverage. The compliance plan is built around business areas: it identifies which [[3-Resources/Glossary/Define Reference Standards|Reference Standards]] are relevant to each one and lets you find all planned audits for a specific business area, effectively acting as the organizing axis for the annual audit schedule.
