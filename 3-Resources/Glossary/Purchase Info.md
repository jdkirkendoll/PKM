## Summary

Purchase Info covers the basic-data prerequisites that must be entered before charge/no-charge outsourcing can be used: the parent part must be an inventory-registered purchase part, the component parts must be entered as inventory-registered sales parts (or non-inventory-registered service parts, with one inventory part mapping to only one sales part), and a product structure can optionally define the components and whether they ship to the supplier free of charge. Getting this basic part/structure data right determines whether the outsourcing purchase order can create its component lines and the resulting customer order correctly.
