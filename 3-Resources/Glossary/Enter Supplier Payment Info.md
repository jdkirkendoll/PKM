## Summary

Supplier payment info covers the fields that control how a supplier gets paid — payment method, payment priority, planned payment delay, and related banking details — entered on the Invoice/Payment area of the Supplier page. This data drives which payment run picks up the supplier's invoices and how the payment is executed (check, bank transfer, bill of exchange, etc.).
