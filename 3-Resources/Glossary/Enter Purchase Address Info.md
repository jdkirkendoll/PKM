## Summary

This is the activity of populating the purchase-specific address on a supplier's Purchase tab — the address purchase orders and purchasing correspondence should be sent to when it differs from the supplier's general address. It's one of several address variants a supplier record can hold (general, purchase, payment) so each business process routes documents to the correct destination.
