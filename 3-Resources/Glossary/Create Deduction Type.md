# Create Deduction Type

## Summary

Sales Contract Deduction is a further reduction taken off the amount applied for on a contract, on top of any discount and retention, before payment is made to the contractor — for example, a statutory training levy withheld and paid on the contractor's behalf (the UK Construction Industry Training Board levy is IFS's own documented example). Deduction Type basic data classifies these deductions (e.g. by statutory levy, customer withholding, or other category) so they can be consistently applied, reported, and reconciled across sales contracts.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The local documentation confirms the Sales Contract Deduction concept and calculation (percentage taken off the amount applied for) in detail, but does not show a distinct "Deduction Type" basic-data screen or its exact classification fields. The classification purpose is inferred from the general deduction concept.
