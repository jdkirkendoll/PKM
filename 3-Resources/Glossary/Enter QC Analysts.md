## Summary

QC analysts are the people entered on the QC Analysts page so they can be recorded as the quality controller against a purchase part. A part's QC analyst is displayed at arrival registration and on several receipt/arrival transactions, letting anyone reviewing the receipt see who is responsible for inspecting that specific part.
