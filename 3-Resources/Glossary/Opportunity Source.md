# Opportunity Source

## Summary

Opportunity Source records the origin of a Business Opportunity — i.e. where the opportunity came from, whether generated directly, converted from a qualified Lead, or created from a Business Activity. It is exposed as an attribute on the Business Opportunity dimension in IFS Cloud's CRM analysis model, and is conceptually the opportunity-level counterpart to the Source field tracked on Leads.
