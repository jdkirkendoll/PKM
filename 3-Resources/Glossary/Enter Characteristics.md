## Summary

Characteristics are configurable attributes (drawn from a defined Characteristic and Characteristic Family) that can be attached to a purchase part to record descriptive or classification data beyond the standard part fields — such as dimensions, material, or grade. They support searching, reporting, and grouping of purchase parts by attributes that matter to a specific business, without requiring custom fields on the part record itself.
