## Summary

A purchase part is the record that adds purchasing-specific data — purchase unit of measure, buying attributes, pricing basis — on top of either an inventory part (for stock-tracked goods) or standalone as a non-inventory-registered purchase part (for goods/services not tracked in stock). It sits in the middle of the standard purchasing setup chain: Inventory Part → Purchase Part → Supplier for Purchase Part, with the last step recording which supplier(s) actually sell the part and at what price. A purchase part cannot exist without either being tied to an inventory part or entered directly as non-inventory.
