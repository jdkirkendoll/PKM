## Summary

Leaving scenarios define the standard sets of steps and final-pay calculations (e.g. outstanding vacation, notice pay, severance) that Payroll runs through when an employment ends, keyed to a reason for leaving. They let termination pay be processed consistently rather than calculated manually for each case.
