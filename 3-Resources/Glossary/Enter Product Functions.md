## Summary
Product Functions are the conceptual building blocks of a product model in IFS Design Standard Product, sitting one level below the product/model in the design hierarchy and one level above the engineering parts that actually implement them. Functions can be arranged into a function structure and organized by discipline (e.g. mechanical vs. electrical views of the same product), and each bottom-level function can have engineering parts — with their own part structures — attached to it, so the product can be designed conceptually before every part choice is finalized. Functions themselves are defined in PDM/Basic Data/Functions, then assigned to a product model and organized in the Product Functions page; each function can also carry its own characteristics. A functional description (a rough document of what the functions need to do) and a later functional specification (a detailed document naming the functions, the product/model, the top part revision, and technical class/attributes) are typically produced in Document Management to support this activity.
