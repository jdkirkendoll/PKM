## Summary

Registers a general/company-level margin percentage to be applied to supplier invoice costs charged to projects, serving as the default that project- or customer-level supplier invoice margins (see [[3-Resources/Glossary/Connect Supplier Invoice Margin to Project|Connect Supplier Invoice Margin to Project]]) can override.
