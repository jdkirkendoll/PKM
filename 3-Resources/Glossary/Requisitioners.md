## Summary

Requisitioners are the people registered as allowed to enter purchase requisitions on behalf of their department. The requisitioner's identity is indicated on the requisition header, and requisitioners can be used as a criterion on purchase requisition authorization rules (rules can be scoped to a specific requisitioner, all user-defined requisitioners, or all system-generated ones). A "requisitioner authorizer" is also one of the six authorizer types — when used, only the requisitioner who created the line can authorize it.
