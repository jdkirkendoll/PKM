## Summary

Custom attributes attached to a Team, following the same general system-defined/user-defined attribute pattern documented for this suite's Resource Attribute Manager (Resource, Shift, and Scheduling attribute types), extended here to the Team object.

> [!note] Confidence: low
> The Attribute Manager pattern is confirmed generally for Resource/Shift/Scheduling objects; its specific application to Teams was not documented and is inferred by analogy.
