## Summary

Not every sale to an EU-country customer needs to appear on your company's Intrastat report — for example, when the tax handling on the customer order line means the receiving party is the one obligated to report (domestic-taxed sales, or cases where the counterpart already reports the movement). This activity flags specific customers so their transactions are left out of the Intrastat collection job, avoiding duplicate or incorrect reporting.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> The Intrastat report's collection logic and its sensitivity to tax liability/domestic-sale scenarios are confirmed generally; the specific "exclude customer" flag and its exact field location were not confirmed in a dedicated source.
