# Product Families

## Summary

Product Family is an optional classification field in the Inventory Part's Classification section, used to group similar parts together — for example to organize parts into product structures in Manufacturing, or to group forecasts in Demand Planning/Master Scheduling. It is entered manually on level-zero (top-level) parts and is one of the accounting/stock-control parameters that can be locked from change once a part carries inventory value. Defining Product Families as basic data before go-live means rental/hire equipment parts can be grouped consistently for forecasting, reporting, and structure-based searches from day one.
