# IFS Connect

## Summary

IFS Connect is IFS Cloud's integration broker for JSON/XML and web-service traffic, used to connect business logic (Integration Projections or PL/SQL) with external processes, applications, and devices. Its openness comes from Transport Connectors — pluggable protocol/format converters hosted inside a Connect Container — so business logic never needs to know which connector delivered a message, and any new connector immediately works with every existing integration. Routing between connectors and business logic is handled entirely through configuration (Routing Rules and Addresses) rather than code, which is what lets "everything talk to everyone" without custom wiring per integration.
