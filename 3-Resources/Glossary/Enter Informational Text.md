## Summary

Informational text is free-form text that can be attached to a purchase part (or a line referencing it) to communicate instructions or notes to whoever processes the purchase — for example handling, storage, or ordering instructions that a buyer or receiving clerk should see. It functions similarly to the generic note capability used elsewhere in IFS Cloud but scoped to the purchase part record.

> [!note] Confidence: moderate
> Inferred from IFS Cloud's general pattern of informational-text fields on part and order records; a dedicated source describing this exact purchase-part activity was not found.
