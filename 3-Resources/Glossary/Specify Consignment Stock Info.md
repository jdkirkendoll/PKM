## Summary

Consignment stock is inventory that physically sits at your warehouse but remains owned by the supplier until it is actually consumed — you're only invoiced (and ownership transfers) at the point of use, not at receipt. Setting up consignment stock for a purchase part/supplier combination requires specific supplier-for-purchase-part settings so receipts land in a consignment-owned status and a periodic consumption/settlement process can invoice the supplier for what was actually used. Combining consignment stock with the charge/no-charge outsourcing flow requires using the charge variant, since consignment parts cannot be shipped as no-charge.
