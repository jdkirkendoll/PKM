## Summary

This activity marks an existing supplier as a template so its non-company-specific information can be copied when quickly registering a brand-new supplier straight from a purchase requisition or purchase order line, instead of setting up every field from scratch. It's enabled either on an individual supplier (Purchase/General sub-tab, "Use as Template" checkbox plus a template description) or in bulk from the Suppliers list. This works together with Quick Supplier Registration, which is what actually creates the new supplier using the template.
