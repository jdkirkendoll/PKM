## Summary

QC Analysts is the basic-data page (in Procurement Basic Data) listing the people who can be assigned as the quality controller for a purchase part. Once entered here, an analyst can be connected to a purchase part and will then show up at arrival registration and on receipt/arrival transactions for that part, identifying who is accountable for its inspection.
