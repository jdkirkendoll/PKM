## Summary

Registers the standard reasons an employment can end (resignation, retirement, dismissal, end of contract, etc.), which are selected when terminating an employee and can also be used to drive [[3-Resources/Glossary/Create Leaving Scenarios|Create Leaving Scenarios]]-type final-pay processing in Payroll and workforce turnover analysis.
