# Source

## Summary

Source (Customer Source / Account Source) is a basic-data value recorded on a Lead that defines the point of contact or channel through which the lead originated — for example Walk In, Trade Fair, Reference, Business Advisor or Business Development. It is configurable per company and is exposed in IFS Cloud's CRM analysis model as the CustomerSource dimension, built on the business activity fact table, letting the organization report on which channels generate the most/best leads.
