## Summary

Net weight is one of the core data points required on the Intrastat report for most parts (the exceptions are parts whose customs statistics number requires a supplementary unit like pieces or liters instead). Entering an accurate net weight on the inventory part ensures the monthly Intrastat submission reports the correct weight for imported/exported goods, as required by EU customs statistics regulations.
