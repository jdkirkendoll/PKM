## Summary

A pallet stock location is where pallets are finally put away once arrival and any required inspection are complete, completing the receipt for pallet-handled parts — the pallet equivalent of a normal picking/stock location. Setting up dedicated pallet stock locations lets a warehouse keep bulk pallet storage physically and administratively separate from piece-pick locations used for individually-handled parts.
