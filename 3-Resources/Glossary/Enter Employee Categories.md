## Summary

Employee category is a user-defined way of grouping employees (traditionally White Collar, Blue Collar, Hourly Paid, Monthly Paid, Union Contracted, etc.) that is usable as a query/selection criterion throughout the system, controls financial posting via dedicated Accounting Rules control types (e.g. Employee Payment, Travel Expense, Project Reporting, and Shop Floor Employee Reporting posting types), and can be used to default information like expense rules or standard costs without maintaining it per employee. Unlike free fields or property codes, it is linked to the employee number and is not date-effective — changing it requires a new employee number to preserve history.
