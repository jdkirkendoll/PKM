## Summary

Scrapping causes are basic data (entered on the Scrapping Causes page) recording why a quantity on a receipt line was scrapped rather than accepted during inspection — for example, damage discovered in the warehouse. Scrapped parts can be scrapped for no credit (the financial burden falls on your company) or scrapped for credit (the burden falls on the supplier), and the scrapping cause is what documents the reason for that classification and for any related supplier returns reporting.
