# Representatives

## Summary

Representatives, in the context of a Lead, refers to assigning one or more sales/business representatives to work the lead — done as one of the first steps after entering a lead's general information, alongside contacts, part lines and competitors. Representative records themselves are maintained as CRM basic data and are reused across leads, opportunities and business activities to track who owns or is working a given record; in IFS Cloud's CRM analysis model these are exposed via the Business Representative / Main Representative dimensions.
