# Enter Lead Time Information on Inventory Part

## Summary

This activity sets the lead time and lead time code for an inventory part, entered on the Acquisition tab of the Inventory Part page (or via the dedicated Enter Lead Time Information function). The lead time code, together with the part's other planning attributes, determines whether MRP creates a shop order proposal, purchase requisition, or production schedule to satisfy demand, and the lead time value drives the start/due dates calculated for whatever proposal is generated. If no expected lead time is entered, the system falls back to the part's purchase or manufacturing lead time, so this data must be correct before MRP can produce reliable order timing.
