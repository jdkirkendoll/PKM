## Summary

Defines the career potentiality levels (e.g. limited, moderate, high potential) that a manager can assign to an employee via the Employee Career Potential page; combined with performance scores, these levels position employees within the N-box Performance and Potential Matrix used for succession planning and talent-pool identification.
