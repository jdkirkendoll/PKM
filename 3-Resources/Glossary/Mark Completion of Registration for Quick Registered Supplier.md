## Summary

Quick Supplier Registration lets a user enter a new supplier quickly and minimally — often by copying a template supplier — so it can immediately be used on a request for quotation, purchase requisition, or purchase order. This activity is the follow-up step of completing that supplier's full registration (filling in the remaining required fields, such as tax and payment data) once there's time, so the quickly-registered supplier is brought up to the same completeness standard as a normally-entered one.
