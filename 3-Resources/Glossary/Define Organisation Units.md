## Summary

Organization units (org codes) are the nodes of the organization tree used for employee assignments, cost/expense postings (each unit can carry its own code-part set), and access control (supervisors are granted access over specific units, optionally including subordinate units). A company organization structure is exclusive to one company; a matrix structure can be shared across companies.
